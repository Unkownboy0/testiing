# Geetorus — Pricing Hypotheses & Packaging Models

## 1. Monetization Strategy & Packaging Tiers

Geetorus employs an **Open-Core & Cloud Managed Control Plane** commercial model.

| Tier | Price Hypothesis | Target Segment | Included Features & Limits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Community (Open Source)** | **Free / Self-Hosted** | Individual developers & open-source contributors | • Unlimited local agents & tasks<br>• Embedded PostgreSQL<br>• Local execution workspace<br>• Full MCP tool gateway |
| **Pro / Team** | **$49 / seat / month** | High-growth startups & agile software teams | • Multi-user collaboration & role RBAC<br>• Cloud execution sandboxes (e2b / Daytona)<br>• Automated database snapshots & backups<br>• Standard email & Discord support |
| **Enterprise** | **Custom ($1,500+ / mo)** | Mid-market & enterprise engineering orgs | • On-premise self-hosted deployment license<br>• SSO (SAML / Okta) & audit log export<br>• Custom MCP tool connectors & dedicated SLA<br>• Air-gapped local model support |
| **Marketplace Fee** | **15% revenue share** | Template creators & prompt engineers | • Revenue share on paid company blueprints & skill packs sold via `geetorus-market` |
